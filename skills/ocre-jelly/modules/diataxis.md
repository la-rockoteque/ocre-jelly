## Diátaxis
- Every page is exactly one type: tutorial (learning, one guided path), how-to (a task, for someone who already knows the basics), reference (facts, complete and dry), or explanation (the why).
- Don't mix types. Move explanation out of a how-to, and steps out of a reference, into their own pages, then link them.
- Title patterns: tutorials "Build your first …", how-tos "How to …", reference named after the thing, explanation "About …" or "Why …".
