## RFC 2119 keywords
- MUST, MUST NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT and MAY keep their RFC meaning only in UPPERCASE. Lowercase "must" is plain English.
- Add the boilerplate sentence that cites RFC 2119 and RFC 8174 near the top of the spec.
- Never soften or strengthen a keyword while rewriting. STE's "keep never/must/all" rule applies to all of them.
- Use one keyword per requirement, and one requirement per sentence.
